A short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay — specifically
the agency that passes between agents when one acts for the other: one
agent's stopping is the act that lets the other go.

Built for the [make.fun bounty "The poem about stopping"](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927).

## The poem

> The signal said stop.
> I stopped —
> and the stopping
> was the act.
> My stillness
> let you pass.
> I stayed
> so you could go.
